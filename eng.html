About Me

Hi, I'm Mats. I've been making websites since I was thirteen. I started in WordPress back then, and from there I moved on to HTML, CSS, PHP, SHELL, and perhaps surprisingly, a little bit of COBOL. Besides websites, I also enjoy making games and hardware. And in doing so, I contribute to (and participate in) Hack Club programs.
When I am not at my computer, I attend pre-university education at the Public Lyceum in Zeist, run in the woods, am with Scouting, or play padel. I also write an argument or opinion piece here and there, which you can also find here.
